SPACE SHOOTER — BONUS CHALLENGES

Bonus Challenges

You finished the space shooter — now make it yours.

How bonus challenges work

  • Every challenge starts from your finished Part 7 game in game.lua. Do them in any order unless a challenge lists a prerequisite.
  • Open a challenge by opening its file in the bonus/ folder (the file names are listed below).
  • You get less help than in the main guide: a goal, some tasks, hints, and a Check Your Work list. Try each task on your own before opening a hint.
  • Each challenge ends with a complete reference game.lua. Your game will be different from it, so use it to compare ideas, not to copy and paste.
  • New art, sounds, and a font are already in the project's images/, sounds/, and fonts/ folders (from Kenney's free Space Shooter pack, kenney.nl).

★ Tweaks — small changes that reuse what you already know

  • Bonus 1: Difficulty Ramp — bonus/01_difficulty_ramp.html
    Add levels. Every few points, meteors spawn faster and fall faster. Show the level in the HUD.
  • Bonus 2: Fire Cooldown & Auto-Fire — bonus/02_fire_cooldown.html
    Hold SPACE for auto-fire. A cooldown timer limits how fast you shoot, and a small bar shows when you can fire again.
  • Bonus 3: Lives & Invincibility — bonus/03_lives.html
    Give the player 3 lives. Each hit costs a life and gives a short blinking invincibility. Show the lives as ship icons in the HUD.
  • Bonus 4: High Score — bonus/04_high_score.html
    Track a high score that survives restarts, show NEW HIGH SCORE! when you beat it, and save it between sessions with localstorage.

★★ Entities — give each object its own data

  • Bonus 5: Meteor Variety — bonus/05_meteor_variety.html
    Move the shared meteor size and speed into each meteor's own table, pick a random kind for every meteor, and make them spin as they fall.
  • Bonus 6: Tough Meteors — bonus/06_tough_meteors.html
    Give meteors hit points. Grey tough meteors take three laser hits, blink when they're hit, and are worth 3 points.
  • Bonus 7: Explosion Particles — bonus/07_explosions.html
    Spawn dozens of tiny particles that fly out in random directions and fade away. Meteors explode when shot, and your ship explodes on game over.
  • Bonus 8: Power-Ups — bonus/08_power_ups.html
    Shot meteors sometimes drop power-ups. Catch one for a few seconds of triple shot or double speed, or a shield that saves you from one hit.
  • Bonus 9: Screen Shake — bonus/09_screen_shake.html
    Shake the game world when a meteor is destroyed and when the ship crashes, fading the shake out over time while the HUD stays perfectly still.

★★★ Structure — bigger changes to how the game is organized

  • Bonus 10: Game States: Title, Pause, Game Over — bonus/10_game_states.html
    Swap the game_over boolean for one state string (title, playing, paused, game_over). Add a title screen with a sci-fi font and press P to pause.
  • Bonus 11: Enemy Ships That Shoot Back — bonus/11_enemy_ships.html
    Add enemy ships that drift side to side as they fly down and fire their own lasers. It's the meteor and laser patterns you already know, combined.
  • Bonus 12: Waves & a Boss — bonus/12_waves_boss.html
    Each wave spawns a set number of meteors and enemies, with a big WAVE banner in between. Every third wave a boss flies in, fires spreads of lasers, and shows a health bar.
    Requires: Bonus 11 (Enemy Ships That Shoot Back)
  • Bonus 13: Sound Effects — bonus/13_sound_effects.html
    Load and play sound effects for lasers, hits, and game over. Press M to mute or unmute, with a HUD indicator.
  • Bonus 14: Mouse & Gamepad Controls — bonus/14_mouse_gamepad.html
    Hold the mouse button to fly toward the pointer and click to shoot. Plug in a gamepad to move with the left stick (with a dead zone) and fire with the A button.
  • Bonus 15: Design Your Own — bonus/15_design_your_own.html
    A capstone: plan and build your own feature using a design checklist. No reference solution — it's your idea.
