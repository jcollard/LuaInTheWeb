# Your focus: can a 10-year-old read and follow this alone?

Read the guide as a 10-year-old who is reading carefully but on their own, with no adult to ask. They have done the earlier parts of the guide, and nothing else. Find every place where the wording would slow them down or lose them.

Look for:
- Sentences that are too long or have several ideas packed into them.
- Programming words used before they're explained, or explained with other programming words ("a table of records", "returns", "derived value", "AABB").
- Math that's used without saying what it does in plain words (`math.floor`, `%`, `math.sin`, radians).
- The same thing called by different names in different places ("function", "helper", "method"; "laser", "bullet", "shot").
- Idioms, figures of speech, or cultural references a kid (or a non-native English speaker) might not get.
- Instructions where it's unclear exactly which function or section the code goes in, or what it goes after.
- Places where the student must hold too much in their head at once.
- Abstract explanations that would be clearer as a concrete example from their own game (their ship, their meteors).

Suggest simpler wording that keeps the full meaning. Don't make it babyish, because 13 and 14 year olds read this too.
